\chapter*{Notation and Conventions}
Throughout the starter material, $K_n(j;N)$ is defined by the ordinary generating function, $k_n(N-2j,N)=n!K_n(j;N)$, $\Phi_N=(K_n(j;N))$, $B_N=\operatorname{diag}(\binom Nj)$, and $S_N=\Phi_NB_N$. All matrix indices begin at zero. In the standard column-vector basis,
$R_Ne_j=(N-j)e_{j+1}$, $L_Ne_j=je_{j-1}$, and $A_N=R_N+L_N$.
The diagonal matrix $\Lambda_N$ has entries $N-2j$.
These conventions, including the distinction between $\Phi_N$ and
$S_N$, are developed in Sections~\ref{sec:finite-sl2-matrices},
\ref{sec:matrix-Phi-definition}, and~\ref{sec:matrix-S-definition}.
Appendix~\ref{app:matrix-tables} contains verified examples for
$N=1,\ldots,14$. Physicists' and probabilists' Hermite normalizations must be explicitly distinguished. Binomial coefficients outside their usual integer range vanish.
The binomial weights are $w_N(j)=2^{-N}\binom Nj$ and
$w_{N,p}(j)=\binom Njp^jq^{N-j}$, $q=1-p$. The biased family is
$Q_n^{(p)}(j;N)$ of Section~\ref{sec:13-2}; the source convention
$K_n(x,N)$ of Chapter~\ref{chap:pq-source-summary} is kept distinct.
Hermite polynomials: $\operatorname{He}_n$ is the probabilists' family,
$H_n(x,t)$ the two-variable heat polynomials of Section~\ref{sec:11-2}.
The letter $H$ alone denotes $F+G$; boldface $\mathbf H$ denotes the Cartan differential operator of Section~\ref{sec:differential-sl2-dictionary}; the oscillator Hamiltonian is
$\mathcal H=\frac12(x^2-D^2)$.

\section*{Dictionary for the integrated Kocik article}
The notation of \cite{KocikFeynman} is translated rather than adopted.
Its total order $n$, row $p$ and column $q$ become our $N,n,j$;
its Krawtchouk matrix $K$ becomes $\Phi_N$, its Kac matrix $M$ becomes
$A_N$, and its eigenvalue matrix becomes $\Lambda_N$.
The source's diagonal binomial metric $\Gamma$ becomes $B_N$;
its Pascal matrix $B$ becomes the already established $P_N$.
The source's operator $N=[L,R]$ becomes $\Lambda_N$ on the fixed-order
space, not the scalar order $N$. We use the ordinary commutator
$[X,Y]=XY-YX$, distinguishing it from the source's half-commutator.
The symbols $\Phi_N$, $S_N=\Phi_NB_N$, and $U_N$ retain their distinct
meanings throughout. The parity matrix is $\sigma_N$; powers-of-two weights
in Pascal coordinates are $W_N$.

\section*{Fixed finite-matrix notation}
The approved level-$N$ symbols are $A_N,R_N,L_N,\Lambda_N,\Phi_N,
J_N,\sigma_N,B_N,S_N,T_N$. Subscripts may be suppressed at fixed level.
Here $A_N=R_N+L_N$, $\Lambda_N=[L_N,R_N]$,
$J_Ne_j=e_{N-j}$, $\sigma_Ne_j=(-1)^je_j$, and $S_N=\Phi_NB_N$.
The matrix $\Phi_N$ is defined by \eqref{eq:gen}.
The symbol $T_N$ is reserved for the skew-symmetric anticommuting
matrix of \eqref{eq:ency-T-definition}; it is not a transition matrix
or the full tensor Hadamard transform. We denote those by
$\mathsf P_N=A_N/N$ and $H^{\otimes N}$, respectively.
