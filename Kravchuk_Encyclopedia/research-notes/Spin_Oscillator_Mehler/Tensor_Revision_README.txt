From Finite Spin to the Harmonic Oscillator
Symmetric-tensor and multinomial revision
========================================
The PDF has 18 pages. The standalone amsart source embeds its bibliography.
Earlier Lecture_Notes_Spin_Oscillator files are left unchanged.

Organizing construction:
independent sites -> symmetric tensor powers -> occupation counts ->
joint input-output multinomial table -> joint/marginal density ratio ->
local Gaussian limit -> Mehler kernel -> oscillator via Gaussian conjugation.

The final lecture derives q-1 oscillators from q reversible states,
including the normal-mode coordinates, multivariate Krawtchouk generating
function, polynomial norms, eigenvalues, Hermite limits, product Mehler
kernels, sl(q)-to-boson contraction, and a three-state/two-oscillator example.

The Fourier sign convention, the explicit finite bilinear kernel, and
both supplied bibliography corrections are retained. Two Griffiths
references were added after bibliographic verification by title/DOI.

Verification: compiled and structurally checked; representative pages
rendered and inspected. Three-state normal-mode orthonormality,
multinomial polynomial norms, and generator eigenvalues were tested
numerically for N=5 and degrees through 2.
