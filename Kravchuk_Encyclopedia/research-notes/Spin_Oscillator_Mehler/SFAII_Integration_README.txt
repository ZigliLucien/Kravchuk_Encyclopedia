SFA II framework integration
============================
The current tensor/multinomial lecture notes now begin from the SFA II
induced-matrix construction, with its composition identity, explicit table
sum, multiplicative spectrum, invariant multinomial law, and generator.
Cumulative Bernoulli trials are presented as a particular one-site matrix.
The Gaussian/Mehler/oscillator analysis is identified as a further limiting
continuation, with reversibility and convergence assumptions distinguished.
Matrix composition, polynomial linearization, and radial convolution are
explicitly distinguished.
