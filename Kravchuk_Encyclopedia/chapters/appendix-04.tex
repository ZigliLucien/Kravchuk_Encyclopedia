\chapter{Multivariate Extensions}
\label{app:multivariate}
\section{Remarks on the multivariate case}
Two developed parts of the Encyclopedia supply the multivariate
framework. Section~\ref{sec:5-3} obtains multivariate Krawtchouk
polynomials from product martingales of independent finite-valued
variables. Section~\ref{sec:ency-sfa-ii} obtains the corresponding
multinomial chains as induced matrices: a one-site $q\times q$ matrix
$P$ induces $P^{[N]}$ on occupation vectors, with
$(PQ)^{[N]}=P^{[N]}Q^{[N]}$. When $P$ is reversible with eigenvalues
$1,r_1,\ldots,r_{q-1}$, the induced eigenvalues are
$r_1^{\nu_1}\cdots r_{q-1}^{\nu_{q-1}}$ with $|\boldsymbol\nu|\le N$,
and the eigenfunctions are multivariate Krawtchouk polynomials.
Tables, explicit low-degree families, and the multivariate Mehler limit
remain to be developed here.
