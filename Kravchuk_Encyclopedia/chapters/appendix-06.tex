\section{Biographical chronology}
The dates below summarize the sourced account in the preceding section.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{rl}
1892 & Born 27 September in Chovnitsy, Volyn.\\
1901 & Family moves to Lutsk.\\
1910 & Completes schooling in Lutsk with a gold medal.\\
1914 & Graduates from the University of Kyiv (St Vladimir University).\\
1924 & Defends doctoral dissertation.\\
1925 & Receives the title of professor.\\
1929 & Introduces the polynomials bearing his name; elected a full\\
     & member of the Ukrainian Academy of Sciences.\\
1938 & Arrested and sentenced on political charges.\\
1942 & Dies 9 March in a forced-labour camp in Kolyma.\\
1956 & Posthumously rehabilitated.\\
1992 & Academy membership restored.
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
