\section{Normalization and numerical verification}
The matrices were generated from the ordinary-generating-function
convention \eqref{eq:gen}, with exact integer entries, and checked against
$\Phi_N^2=2^NI$ and $S_N^T=S_N$. Eigenvalues were computed by a symmetric
double-precision eigensolver. The largest relative residual
$\max|S_Nv-\lambda v|/\max|\lambda|$ over all listed orders was
$1.0\times10^{-15}$.
Because $S_N^2\ne cI$ in general, these spectra are not those of the
orthogonal involution $U_N$, whose eigenvalues are $\pm1$.
